# Sequence IQ Puzzle Content

Each platform owns and maintains its own copy of this file.

V1 content allocation:

## Easy

20 series (E01–E20)

## Hard

20 series (H01–H20)

## Master

20 series (M01–M20)

## Content Format

Each series will contain:

- Series ID
- Difficulty
- Number sequence
- Four answer options
- Correct answer
- Optional hint material for the three hint stages

Puzzle content must remain plain text and must not depend on a database.

Full V1 puzzle questions will be populated during the content-building phase.
