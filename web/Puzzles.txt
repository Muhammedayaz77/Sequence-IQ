# Sequence IQ Puzzle Content

Each platform owns and maintains its own copy of this file.

V1 content allocation:

## Easy
20 series (E01–E20)

## Hard
20 series (H01–H20)

## Master
20 series (M01–M20)

## Content Format
Each series will contain:
- Series ID
- Difficulty
- Number sequence
- Four answer options
- Correct answer
- Optional hint material for the three hint stages

Puzzle content remains plain text and is independent of application databases.
